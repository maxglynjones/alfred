Device readings & recovery

R1 readings need a ring identity and battery supplied by Even. If missing, briefly place the ring on its charger, remove it, then refresh. This may prompt a status update; it cannot guarantee that Even exposes the reading. No pairing reset is needed.

R1 health statistics are not exposed by the installed Even SDK. Use Even’s Health page for those readings.

R1 identity recovery

If Even sends an unidentified ring reading, enter the exact R1 serial from its device information or packaging once. This binds readings from that serial only and stays in the Even app's local storage. Never enter the glasses serial.

Connection & help

Setup guides

  1. Install the Alfred integration in Home Assistant. Keep Home Assistant available.
  2. Open Home Assistant → Alfred, choose speech and optional AI, then add optional services.
  3. Use HA Cloud remote access or a private connection such as Tailscale. Build for your HTTPS address, upload through your own Even Hub app, and install your current test/beta build.
  4. Create a pairing code in Home Assistant → Alfred → Glasses & access and enter it here once.
  5. Move Alfred first in the Even menu; assign ring long press to Menu. Hold, release, tap to open.

To leave: tap then hold for the stock contextual menu, then Close Alfred. Or use Manual controls below.

Network error? Check your remote connection and Home Assistant first. Pair again only if your code or saved pairing is invalid. No need to reset the glasses.

Google/Outlook account setup, model settings and Home Assistant device approval live in Home Assistant → Alfred.

For iPhone notifications and WhatsApp: this app can summarise connected inboxes and your shared text, but cannot browse other apps’ iOS notifications or a personal WhatsApp inbox.

Manual controls

Launch: hold to open the stock menu, then tap Alfred (first item). Exit: tap then hold to open the contextual menu and choose Close Alfred, or use Close Alfred here. Double-tap uses your configured gesture profile: system exit in Store mode; ring/right stem dismiss and left stem toggles Dashboard in Personal mode. Hold to speak; release to submit. The small microphone icon stays visible while listening. Tap or swipe up to dismiss a reply. Swipe down to read more.
