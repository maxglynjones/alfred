# Product demonstration

Suggested sequence: launch Alfred from the stock menu; hold to ask a short question; control one light; display a shopping list; schedule a reminder; show the dashboard; send a research report to yourself. Finish with the Quick Start and optional connector tiles.

Capture genuine device behaviour and timing. Use disposable demonstration accounts/data and one clearly labelled test light. Mention that Home Assistant and a user-hosted reachable backend are required. Avoid claiming a firmware replacement, direct custom ring shortcut, always-on wake word or phone-call support.
